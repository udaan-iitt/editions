In their gifted hands, I see a glowing gem,\
Singing their glory in a triumphant hum.\
Lighting up their faces with an elusive radiance,\
It has a rare hue, mirroring the bearer’s brilliance.\
Their proud eyes meet my desolate gaze,\
Stirring regrets within my tangled maze.\
Watching them hold it close to their heart,\
My thoughts wander off to lives I could never start.\
All that I’ve seen,\
Floods my mind with thoughts of everything I could have been.\
So I stand there, stretching out my empty hand,\
Hoping my long – lost gem will land.
