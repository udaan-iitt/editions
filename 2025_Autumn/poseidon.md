Why? O, Poseidon, why?\
Why do you have to be so mean?\
Gentle, frothy waves and a clear blue sky,\
Isn't this for you, a picturesque scene?\

Diving into the soothing icy waters,\
A visit to Nemo’s anemone,\
The gentle chaos as a fish shoal scatters,\
Isn't this how a sea must be shown?\

Why then? O, Poseidon, why?\
Why do you have to be, so evil, a god?\
Raging tall waves and a dark black sky,\
Won’t this make Atlantis a chaotic abode?\

Dark, creepy shadows in the mighty sea,\
Lightning and thunder, a dramatic ace,\
A monster, Kraken or Great White, may it be,\
Won't this make the sea a treacherous place?\

Thalassophilic or thalassophobic thoughts though,\
The sea is a pretty curious place to be.\
All these daunting questions, let me know.\
The great Poseidon, why, they call thee!
