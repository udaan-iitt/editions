To anyone reading this, I hope you are doing well. I wish to share a brief account of my summer internship experience at the **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)** in **New Delhi**. For your convenience, I’ve divided this into various sections that you can refer to individually.

- - -

### About the Organisation

The **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)** is the National Standards Body of India. My internship was situated within the **Water Resources Division (WRD) Sectional Committee 9** at the **BIS Headquarters**.

**WRD 09** is the "Dams and Spillways Sectional Committee," which is a crucial group responsible for developing and publishing **Indian Standards (IS)** related to the design, construction and safety of dams, spillways diversion works across the nation.

- - -

### Application Process

My selection for the internship was facilitated through my college, the **Indian Institute of Technology, Tirupati**. The selection was entirely merit-based, primarily considering my **CGPA**.

**Essential documents included:**
- My **Resume/CV**
- A **No-Objection Certificate (NOC)** from my department
- Some additional forms that had to be uploaded to the **POD**.

Once the initial selection and approval were complete, I was formally accepted to begin research on the new work item "Hydraulic Design of Stepped Spillways - Guidelines".

- - -

### Experience at BIS

My internship started on the **19th of May** and lasted until the **14th of July 2024**. Over the eight weeks, I was given the key project **"Hydraulic Design of Stepped Spillways - Guidelines"** to work upon.

The experience was highly structured and provided both government and industry exposure:

- **Initial Phase (2 weeks - BIS HQ):** I worked from the Headquarters under my mentors, **Shri Vaibhav Jindal** and **Shri Vipul Anand**, to prepare the initial **Pre-Standardisation Report** and the **Working Draft** for the new guideline.
- **Research & Field Phase (4 weeks - Remote/Field):** This period was dedicated to extensive research and two invaluable field visits:
    - **Sardar Sarovar Dam:** I visited the facility and control room, gathering insights from engineers on the dam's design, operation and maintenance.
    - **Central Water and Power Research Station (CWPRS):** I toured nearly all departments, including Structural Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering, Rock Engineering, Water Resources Engineering, etc., learning the various **processes, tests and model studies** conducted for dam projects.
- **Compilation Phase (2 weeks - BIS HQ):** I returned to the Headquarters to compile all the research, finalize my work and submit both the final documents.

- - -

### Pros

- **Financial Support:** It was a **paid internship**, offering a **₹20,000 monthly stipend**. Additionally, all **travel and accommodation charges were fully reimbursed** for the field trips, which significantly eased the financial burden.
- **National Impact:** You directly contribute to formulating a **National Standard**, impacting India's future water resource infrastructure.
- **Unique Exposure:** The ability to conduct crucial field visits to premier facilities like the **Sardar Sarovar Dam** and **CWPRS** is unparalleled.
- **Exceptional Mentorship:** The scientists and consultants at BIS are genuinely brilliant and take the time to guide you throughout the technical process.

- - -

### Cons

- **Pace of Work:** Being a government organisation, the process of standardisation is systematic and deliberate, meaning things sometimes take time to complete.
- **Paperwork:** There was quite a bit of necessary administrative and documentation work involved in coordinating the research and field trips.

- - -

In a nutshell, if you are interested in **research** and want to contribute toward the nation by helping in **formulating standards**, the Bureau of Indian Standards is highly recommended.

**Overall, 8/10 – would highly recommend it!**
