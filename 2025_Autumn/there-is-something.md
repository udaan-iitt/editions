There’s something I can’t quite say,\
Won’t you feel it anyway?\
This restless heart, this silent plea—\
Can’t you understand what’s wrong with me? <br>

When from the world I lose the fight,\
You’re the one that feels so right.\
But why, oh why, does it feel so?\
Please tell me, love, I need to know. <br>

When we’re apart, you're on my mind,\
In dreams, it’s only you I find.\
And when I’m near, my eyes just stay—\
On you, and never drift away. <br>

Your silence cuts, it makes me ache,\
But be the cure, for my heart's sake.\
I still recall that golden day,\
When first we met—love found its way. <br>

A flower bloomed within my soul,\
Its scent still makes my spirit whole.\
So like that bloom, just let love grow,\
And let your warmth again bestow. <br>

Today, I live with thoughts of you,\
Each dream I weave, each sky I view.\
But when I’m gone, and silence calls,\
You’ll wish you spoke—inside those walls. <br>

Don’t let regret come claim your heart,\
Let’s kill the pain before it starts.\
Come in my arms, just hold me tight,\
Let love dissolve our every fight. <br>

Forget the wounds, the scars, the blame,\
Let’s set alight love’s dying flame.\
Say those words, so deep, so true—\
The ones I long to hear from you. <br>

Just come to me… I’m waiting so,\
Oh darling, please… now let love flow.
