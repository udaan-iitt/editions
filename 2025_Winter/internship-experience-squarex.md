<p>My internship at SquareX was a significant milestone in my professional journey, providing me with invaluable experiences and insights into the fields of cybersecurity and software development.</p>

<h1>Connecting with SquareX</h1>
<p>My introduction to SquareX came through Dr. Ajin, who recognized my potential and facilitated my application. The selection process I underwent was unique compared to typical internships; while I was directly selected post an interview, most candidates engaged in a rigorous assessment involving 6-7 tasks that tested their skills across various domains, including Docker and Chrome native messaging followed by a second interview. This approach not only evaluates technical expertise but also simulates real-world problem-solving scenarios in cybersecurity.</p>
<br>

<h1>Preparing for the Interview</h1>
<p>Preparation for the interview was rooted in my passion for cybersecurity and software development. I had participated in numerous hackathons and personal projects, which provided practical experience and enhanced my problem-solving skills. Unlike many peers who might have relied solely on academic performance, my strategy involved intense last-minute study sessions that helped me grasp concepts quickly. This proactive learning style proved beneficial during the interview, allowing me to demonstrate both knowledge and adaptability.</p>
<br>

<h1>Goals During the Internship</h1>
<p>Setting clear objectives was crucial for maximizing my internship experience. My primary goals included:</p>

<ul>
<li>Establishing a positive reputation within SquareX.</li><br>

<li>Absorbing as much knowledge as possible about the industry.</li><br>

<li>Pushing my boundaries by exploring unfamiliar areas.</li><br>

<li>Producing high-quality work while observing the dynamics of a startup environment.</li><br>
</ul>

<p>These objectives guided my actions and decisions throughout the internship, ensuring I remained focused on personal and professional growth.</p>
<br>

<h1>Navigating Challenges</h1>
<p>One of the most significant challenges I faced was adapting to remote work while managing time zone differences with Singapore Standard Time (SGT). The asynchronous nature of our distributed team often meant delays in receiving feedback on projects, which tested my patience and independence. Additionally, working in a startup required flexibility as roles and responsibilities shifted frequently. This dynamic environment demanded that I remain agile and responsive to changing needs.</p>
<br>

<h1>Experiencing Singapore</h1>
<p>Although I primarily worked remotely from college, I had the opportunity to travel to Singapore briefly during my internship. This experience was enriching; I stayed in comfortable hotels and explored the city during weekends. Singapore's cleanliness and organization left a strong impression on me, though I noted a more formal atmosphere compared to other cultures.</p>
<br>

<h1>Financial Aspects</h1>
<p>While I cannot disclose specific financial details regarding stipends or potential pre-placement offers (PPOs), I can affirm that the internship provided substantial industry exposure and networking opportunities that are invaluable for career development, and I was offered a PPO.</p>
<br>

<h1>Memorable Moments</h1>
<p>Throughout my time at SquareX, several memorable moments stand out. One particularly enjoyable experience involved breaking down cultural stereotypes with colleagues Si Wei and Samuel, who had preconceived notions about India. To add humor to our discussions, Si Wei surprised us by knowing a few Hindi phrases! Additionally, during my stay in Singapore, I reconnected with Nandhavardhan Namboori, who showcased unexpected charm towards a girl during a late-night swim at the hotel pool.</p>
<br>

<h1>Advice for Future Interns</h1>
<p>For those looking to intern in similar organizations, here are some key pieces of advice:</p>

<ul>
<li>Build a strong foundation: Focus on understanding core concepts rather than relying solely on guidance from others.</li><br>

<li>Be patient: Opportunities may take time to materialize; persistence is key.</li><br>

<li>Embrace learning: Engage deeply with projects to maximize your learning experience.</li><br>
</ul>

<p>I personally waited about a year before securing this internship; resilience and dedication were essential throughout this journey.</p>

<p>In conclusion, my internship at SquareX was an enriching experience that equipped me with both technical skills and professional insights. It reinforced the importance of adaptability, continuous learning, and building meaningful connections within the industry. I encourage fellow students to embrace every opportunity—each experience is a stepping stone toward your future career!</p>
