Introduction and Early ContextThe conversation opens by setting the stage for a historical and technical exploration of India’s defense technology advancements. The focus lies on the early 1970s—a period marked by geopolitical and scientific milestones, including the aftermath of the 1971 Indo-Pak war and the inception of India’s nuclear ambitions, such as the Smiling Buddha project. Against this backdrop, the Defence Research and Development Laboratory (DRDL) under the aegis of the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) embarked on groundbreaking projects like Project Devil and Project Valiant. These initiatives aimed to bolster India’s strategic military and defense capabilities in response to regional threats and evolving global defense paradigms.
